About

I'm John, a London-based videographer, photographer and editor with over 12 years of experience turning ideas into content that stands out.

From fashion and music to sport and lifestyle, I've worked with brands and organisations including Topman, the Rugby Football Union (RFU) and Westminster City Council, alongside artists such as Maya Jane Coles, Marina & the Diamonds and Kim Ann Foxman.

I love creating bold, engaging videos and photography that capture attention and tell a story. From the first shot to the final edit, I get stuck into the whole creative process, whether I'm behind the camera on my own or working with a bigger crew.
