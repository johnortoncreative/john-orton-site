About

I’m John, a London-based videographer, photographer and editor with more than 12 years’ experience creating content that stands out.

My work spans fashion, music, sport and lifestyle. I’ve worked with brands and organisations including Topman, the Rugby Football Union (RFU) and Westminster City Council, with artists such as Maya Jane Coles, Marina & the Diamonds and Kim Ann Foxman, and with well-known personalities including Big John Fisher, Jack Fincham and Gaz Beadle of Geordie Shore.

I love making bold, engaging videos and photos that grab attention and tell a story. I’m hands-on through the whole creative process, from the first shot to the final edit, whether I’m working solo behind the camera or as part of a larger crew.
